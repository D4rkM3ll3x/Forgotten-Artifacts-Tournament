The Forgotten Artefacts

Relics of a forgotten age lie scattered across Gladius Prime, ready to be (ab)used.

Archaicopia

Restores +0.5 hitpoints to your units each turn.

Drains 1 Victory Point from each enemy device every turn.

Siren Caster

Increases the loyalty of your cities by +2.

Drains 1 Victory Point from each enemy device every turn.

Ancient Pathcutter

Increases the movement of one allied non-flyer unit by +2 for 5 turns.

Drains 1 Victory Point from each enemy device every turn.

Full Spectrum Analyser

Reveals tiles in radius 2 around a tile that is already visible.

Drains 1 Victory Point from each enemy device every turn.

Vaul Domination Device

Every player starts the game with a Vaul Domination Device with 180 hit points. Its action points show your own remaining points, its movement shows your opponents'. When a team's device reaches 0 hit points, that team loses the game.

Shoutout to YurGa for letting me use his Victory Point system.

Edict I

Tournament Structure

1.1 Main Event

The tournament is played as a Swiss system with up to five rounds. Ten qualified teams plus any Wildcard teams take part. In a Swiss system, no team is knocked out: every team plays every round, so up to five matches in total. Pairings are made according to each team's current record. The target pace is one round per week.

1.2 Open Qualifier

If more than ten teams register, an open qualifier is held. To qualify, a team must play at least one game with the tournament mod. The ten best-ranked teams advance automatically to the Main Event. Ties for the last places are decided by the organizer.

1.3 Wildcards

The organizers may advance additional Wildcard teams, based on their Style Points. Cool plays, unusual strategies and heroes that add to the fun are rewarded.

Edict II

Registration

2.1 Team or solo

Players register either as a fixed team or as a solo player. A team may list any number of players on the registration form, and decides which two of them play each match. Solo players can ask the organizer for help forming a team.

2.2 Faction lock

Each player chooses one faction at registration and is locked to it for the entire tournament. The lock applies to the player, not the team. The in-game name and faction used during the tournament must match the registration.

Both players of a team may choose the same faction, and mirror matches are allowed.

2.3 Replacements

If a player becomes unavailable, the team may bring in a replacement from the pool of registered solo players. The replacement plays their own registered faction. A player may be active for only one team at a time. The organizer may manually approve team switches or assignments. If a team cannot field two players, the match is a forfeit.

Edict III

Gameplay

3.1 General

All matches are played on Standard speed. The lobby settings are preset by the Forgotten Artifacts Tournament Mod and must not be changed.

3.2 Remakes

  • Each team may call one remake per game. The remake must be declared by the end of turn 5.
  • Moderators may allow further remakes in exceptional cases.
  • Wrong lobby settings may be corrected until turn 10 if both teams agree, and after turn 10 only with a moderator's permission.

3.3 Pausing

The game may be paused if both teams agree or a moderator allows it.

3.4 Recording

If no spectator is present, players record their games and send the recordings to the organizer, so matches can be scored and reviewed for Style Points. Streaming is allowed and encouraged.

3.5 Disconnects and crashes

If a player disconnects or the game crashes, the host reloads the latest save and the game continues. If the problem cannot be resolved within a reasonable time, a moderator or the organizer decides the result based on the state of the game.

Edict IV

Lobby Settings

4.1 Mods

The Forgotten Artifacts Tournament Mod must be active and placed at the top of the in-game mod list. No other gameplay mods are needed: all balance changes are already included in the tournament mod.

4.2 Hosting and spectators

  • If a moderator is present, the moderator hosts the game.
  • Otherwise a player who owns all unit DLCs hosts, with priority given to the Team A player.
  • If no tournament host or moderator is present, the match is created as a private lobby with at least three spectator slots for commentary and recording.

Edict V

Simultaneous Turns

  • The game starts with simultaneous turns. Either team or a moderator may call simultaneous turns off at any time. Teams are strongly advised to do so early. While simultaneous turns are active, every action is allowed.
  • Once called off, the host disables simultaneous turns at the start of the next turn. No player may move units on that turn until they are disabled.
  • There is no peace turn: attacks are allowed as soon as simultaneous turns are disabled. Calling them off late can give the opponent an extra turn.
  • Moderators call them off once the teams could meet on the next turn, and earlier where needed to prevent a double turn for Team A.
  • In case of a dispute, the save of the first non-simultaneous turn can be sent to the organizer.
Edict VI

Scheduling

6.1 Pace and contact

The tournament plays one round per week. All scheduling takes place via Discord. Once teams receive their opponent's contact details, they get in touch, schedule the match for the upcoming week and notify the organizer. A team that fails to do so loses the match.

6.2 No-shows

  • A team that does not appear within 15 minutes of the scheduled time without notice loses the match.
  • A team that gives notice may be up to 30 minutes late. Beyond that, the organizer and the opposing team decide whether the match still takes place.

6.3 Scheduling disputes

Moderators settle scheduling disputes. If no moderator is available or a team disagrees with the decision, the organizer has the final say. The organizer may settle a dispute by coin toss, or in favour of the team more likely to play the next match without further delay.

6.4 Reporting results

The winning team posts the result with a screenshot of the end screen in the Forgotten Artifacts Tournament thread of the Gladius tournament channel on Discord, within 24 hours of the match.

Edict VII

Game Disputes

When a moderator is present, they handle in-game disputes first. Teams may appeal to the organizer, who decides based on the available recordings and saves. The organizer has the final say on all disputes.

Edict VIII

Conduct

  • Knowingly exploiting bugs or crashes is not allowed. Suspected bugs are reported to a moderator.
  • Players stay respectful in game chat, voice and on Discord.
  • Using an opponent's live stream for information during a match is not allowed. Streamers are advised to use a delay.
  • The organizer may issue warnings, award losses or disqualify teams for breaches.
Edict IX

Style Points

Style Points are not ranking points. Commentators award them for unusual plays, creative strategies, interesting faction combinations, spectacular kills, rare heroes and entertaining games.

  • In the qualifier, Style Points can influence advancement through Wildcards.
  • In the Main Event, Style Points accumulate over all rounds.
  • The team with the most Style Points is immortalised on the Golden Throne of the tournament mod, together with the overall winner.
